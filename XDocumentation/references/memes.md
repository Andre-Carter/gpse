# GPSE MEMES & MISSION-ROOM LORE

A collection of recurring jokes, phrases, memes, and sacred engineering terminology from the development of GPSE.

---

## 🗿 SACRED PHRASES

### Praise Klang

The traditional blessing of machinery, engineering, Space Engineers energy, and successful GPSE construction.

> **Praise Klang.**

### NO HERESY

Used whenever an idea threatens the architectural integrity, sanity, or sacred engineering principles of GPSE.

> **NO HERESY!**

Variations:

* No heresy detected.
* No heresy in the circuit.
* Absolutely no heresy.
* **NO HERESEY!** — intentionally misspelled variant preserved as historical lore.

### Brother

Standard mission-room designation for the fellow engineer participating in the GPSE operation.

> **Brother, we have a problem.**

> **Brother, I think we're onto something.**

### OH BROTHER

Expression used when a technical rabbit hole has suddenly become much larger than expected.

> **OH BROTHER.**

Especially appropriate immediately before discovering another layer of the architecture.

## ⚙️ REDSTONE ENGINEERING

### The GPSE Redstone Division

The imaginary department responsible for constructing GPSE's increasingly elaborate computational machinery.

Core doctrine:

> Build the redstone.
> Test the redstone.
> Then add more redstone.

### No Creepers

Creepers represent catastrophic architectural destruction.

> **No creepers blowing up our redstone contraptions down the line.**

This became shorthand for:

**Design things properly now so future changes don't destroy the system.**

### Creeper Protocol

Before major architectural changes:

1. Save the work.
2. Check the system.
3. Commit the checkpoint.
4. Proceed carefully.
5. Keep creepers away from the machinery.

## 🔥 THE FORGE

### The Furnace

The experimental development environment.

Ideas go into the furnace before they become permanent architecture.

> **Put the ingots in the furnace.**

Not every experiment belongs in production.

### One Cog at a Time

GPSE should be constructed incrementally rather than attempting the entire machine simultaneously.

> **One cog at a time.**

### The Forge Is Lit

Used when development is progressing particularly well.

## 🤢 DEBUGGING MEMES

### FLARKING NASTY

Used when an intermediate implementation is ugly, confusing, or malformed but reveals something useful.

> **FLARKING NASTY mate.**

### The Beast

## 🛡️ CORE GPSE MEME DOCTRINE

### No One Left Behind

Originally used as mission/personnel status humor.

Engineering interpretation:

> Don't leave important systems, data, or functionality behind accidentally.

### Don't Break Words

A particularly important READ/Bible engineering rule.

> **DONT BREAK WORDS.**

Especially relevant to the typed textual corpus.

### Normalize Commands. Preserve Data.

The CLI principle established during command parsing work.

Commands may be normalized for reliable input.

Data should retain its original meaning and representation.
